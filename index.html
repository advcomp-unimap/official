About Our Centre

The Centre of Excellence for Advanced Computing (AdvComp) at UniMAP is a premier research hub focused on cutting-edge computational technologies.

Our mission is to advance the frontiers of computing through innovative research, collaboration with industry partners, and training the next generation of computing experts.
